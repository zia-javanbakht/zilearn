The Hashin damage initiation criterion considers four potential failure modes, each associated with a specific index: (i) fiber breakage in tension ($F_f^t$), (ii) fiber buckling in compression ($F_f^c$), (iii) matrix cracking in tension ($F_m^t$), and (iv) matrix crushing in compression ($F_m^c$). The corresponding equations for these failure modes are as follows:

$$F_f^t = \left(\frac{\sigma_{11}}{X_T}\right)^2 + \alpha\left(\frac{\sigma_{12}}{S_L}\right)^2 \le 1.0 \quad and \quad \sigma_{11} \ge 0 \tag{8}$$

$$F_f^c = \left(\frac{\sigma_{11}}{X_c}\right)^2 \quad and \quad \sigma_{11} \le 0 \tag{9}$$

$$F_m^t = \left(\frac{\sigma_{22}}{Y_T}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\sigma_{12}}{S_L}\right)^2 \le 1.0 \quad and \quad \sigma_{22} \ge 0 \tag{10}$$

$$F_m^c = \left(\frac{\sigma_{22}}{2S_T}\right)^2 + \left[\left(\frac{Y_C}{2S_T}\right)^2 - 1\right]\frac{\sigma_{22}}{Y_C} + \left(\frac{\sigma_{12}}{S_L}\right)^2 \le 1.0 \quad and \quad \sigma_{22} < 0 \tag{11}$$

In the given equations, $X_T$, $Y_T$, $X_C$, and $Y_C$ denote the tensile and compressive strength along the longitudinal and transverse directions, respectively. The values of $G_{LT}$, $G_{LC}$, $G_{TT}$, and $G_{TC}$ from Table 7 represent the tensile and compressive fracture energies along the longitudinal and transverse directions.
